About This Quiz

Learn how to use AI tools responsibly and protect your information.

What is this quiz?

This quiz helps students recognise when sharing information with AI tools could create privacy or security risks. It uses everyday scenarios to encourage safer decisions.

How does scoring work?

You will answer 10 questions, with three options for each. Answers are worth 0, 1 or 3 points depending on the level of risk. Your final score is out of 30, with feedback explaining each answer.

Is the quiz anonymous?

Yes. You do not need to provide your name or personal details. Results are associated with an assigned team identifier, and the leaderboard displays team averages rather than individual scores.
